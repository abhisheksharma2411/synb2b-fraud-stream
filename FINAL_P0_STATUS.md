## The highest-leverage gaps

1. **The estimator fails its own simplest validation rung.** `S1` of the validation
   ladder — oracle `p_d`, no delay, no censoring, no trimming, no decay, no drift — gives a
   calibration ratio of 0.9883 with a 95% interval of [0.9787, 0.9979] that excludes 1.0.
   Nothing downstream of the estimator can be interpreted until that is explained, because
   every published number inherits it.
2. **P0-1's result is against the hypothesis.** Aligning the cohorts halved the cohort gap
   and made the calibration ratio *worse* (0.877 → 0.829), and did nothing whatever on ULB.
   That has to be explained or the alignment argument does not survive review.
3. **The paper describes a different estimator than the one in the repository.** Every M5
   figure is the misaligned variant's, and nothing about cohorts, alignment or the joint
   likelihood appears in `main.tex` at all.
4. **Two of the review's cheapest asks are still open.** `err_t` remains undefined after two
   uses (P0-7), and the Prop. 1 domain still admits `ε = 1` and constrains the product rather
   than `b` (P0-11). Both are single-line edits.
